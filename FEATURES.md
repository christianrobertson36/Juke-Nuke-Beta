# Juke-Nuke Beta Features

Juke-Nuke is a self-hosted **digital jukebox and entertainment hub** designed for man caves, games rooms, home bars, party spaces, lounges and other shared-room setups.

The goal is to give the room one interface for music, karaoke, video, retro gaming and more, rather than making guests jump between several separate apps.

## Jukebox & music

- Music library and playback
- Album artwork and metadata
- Playlists
- Shared playback state
- Persistent player controls
- Touchscreen-friendly navigation
- Remote / companion interaction

## Karaoke

- Dedicated karaoke section
- Singer workflow
- Queue-style shared use
- Designed for big-screen and party-room use
- Built as a first-class feature, not an afterthought

## Retro & arcade

- Retro / arcade section
- Emulator-focused workflow
- Xbox controller support
- Couch / games-room friendly use
- Designed to sit beside the jukebox within the same interface

## Movies & video

- Movie and video library
- Resume behaviour for supported playback
- Shared playback controls
- TMDB metadata and artwork enrichment
- Local sidecar artwork support

## Radio & IPTV

- Radio playback
- IPTV support
- One-room interface alongside the rest of the entertainment features

## Photos & shared-room media

- Photos
- Shared playback state
- Remote interaction from another device
- TV and big-screen friendly use

## Room-first controls

Juke-Nuke is designed to work as part of the room itself:

- touchscreen
- TV / projector
- wall-mounted display
- home-bar screen
- games-room monitor
- Xbox controller
- companion / remote device

## Why the all-in-one design exists

Juke-Nuke is not simply trying to replace Jellyfin, Navidrome, RomM or other specialist servers one-for-one.

The point is the experience of walking into a dedicated entertainment room and having **one jukebox-style interface** that can handle the night: music, karaoke, retro games, movies, IPTV, radio and shared control.

It is closer to a self-hosted entertainment console than a conventional media server.
